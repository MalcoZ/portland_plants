How to play

Move every card to the four foundations at the top right, building each suit from Ace up to King.

On the table, stack cards downward in alternating colors. The four free cells at top left each hold one card.

The Scene button randomizes the animated background: new colors and new shapes every time.

Tap a card once and it moves to the best spot: a foundation first, then a matching column, then a free cell. To choose the spot yourself, drag the card or run of cards. Moving a run needs enough free cells and empty columns.
